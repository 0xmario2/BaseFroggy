About BaseFroggy

BaseFroggy is not a coin. Not an NFT. Not a game.

BaseFroggy is a movement. A group of people who think the internet should be more fun. We make memes, support artists, and build friendships on Base. Started in 2026 by 3 friends who were tired of serious crypto stuff.
